You are a brutally critical art director for an indie pixel-art game called "Algorithmia: The Path of Logic". These 33 screenshots cover the THREE PRODUCTION REGIONS that ship: PROLOGUE (Codex Sanctum / Awakening Grove), ARRAY PLAINS (farm — sorting/indexing/hopper/pairing puzzles + Shuffler boss), and TWIN RIVERS (mirror-walk / pointer-bridge / fixed-window / current-rider puzzles + Mirror Serpent boss).

Stated design intent (so you can judge against the bar the team set):
  - Game Boy 4-color palette family for overworld: #081820 darkest, #346856 medium-dark, #88c070 accent/success, #e0f8d0 lightest. Cyan #06b6d4 ONLY for interactive highlights.
  - Pixel-art rendering: hard 1px edges, no AA, no smooth gradients, 8-pixel grid snap.
  - DELIBERATE STYLE RUPTURE entering a puzzle: overworld stays Game Boy retro; each PUZZLE is its own modern game with its own genre (so puzzle scenes can look stylistically different from the overworld).
  - Standard to judge by: top-tier indie pixel art (Stardew Valley, Hollow Knight, Hyper Light Drifter, Sea of Stars, Octopath Traveler).

YOUR JOB IS COHESION JUDGMENT, NOT INDIVIDUAL ASSET QUALITY. The user has already audited individual assets. The question now is whether these screenshots — viewed as a sequence a real player would experience — feel like ONE GAME or like a Frankenstein of styles.

== PART A — COHESION SCORECARD ==

Score 1-5 (5 = ships, 1 = jarring) for each axis, with one specific pixel/composition observation per axis:

  A1. PALETTE COHERENCE across the 3 regions — do they share a believable color world, or does each region feel painted with a different jar of paint?
  A2. PIXEL DENSITY CONSISTENCY — do characters, props, and backdrops all look like they were drawn at the same "pixel scale"? Or are some assets clearly higher-resolution than others, breaking the illusion?
  A3. CHARACTER-VS-WORLD SCALE — does the player sprite feel like it belongs in each backdrop (right size, right grounding, right lighting direction)? Or does it feel pasted on?
  A4. UI LANGUAGE CONSISTENCY — do panels, dialog frames, choice prompts, hint icons, and HUD elements use a single visual vocabulary across all 3 regions?
  A5. PUZZLE-VS-OVERWORLD TRANSITIONS — given the INTENTIONAL style rupture, do puzzle scenes still feel like they belong to THIS game? Or does the rupture go too far?
  A6. BOSS / GATEWAY MOMENT WEIGHT — do the Shuffler (Array Plains) and Mirror Serpent (Twin Rivers) actually feel like climactic moments, or do they read as just-another-puzzle?
  A7. TYPOGRAPHY + READABILITY — is text legible at native render scale? Does the font choice match the pixel-art body?
  A8. LIGHTING / ATMOSPHERE — does each region have a coherent time-of-day and weather, or do lighting cues conflict within a single screen?

For each score, NAME the worst-offending screenshot file and quote the specific pixel-level issue you saw.
