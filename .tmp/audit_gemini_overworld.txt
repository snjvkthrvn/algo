### SPECIFIC ASKS

**1. Did Wave 7 (companion + glitch + watcher regen) improve overworld cohesion?**
**Yes, by roughly +0.5 overall.** Replacing the HD painted/anime actors with flat 8-bit sprites that match the player successfully unified the "cast." The Bit companions, the Sorting Farmer, and Professor Node now clearly belong to the same species and resolution class. It was a required fix.

**2. Single biggest REMAINING overworld issue**
The jarring juxtaposition of 8-bit characters and vector backgrounds against **high-resolution, painted HD props**. Look at `27b-twin-rivers-closure.png`: you have a flat, minimalist 8-bit player and companion floating on a clean vector matrix floor, standing directly next to a hyper-detailed, hand-painted mountain portal with textured brown rocks, realistic pine trees, and a glowing HD gold vortex. It shatters any illusion of a cohesive world.

**3. Overworld ship-readiness**
**The overworld is NOT shippable.** The gap to a 3.5/5 is bridging the environmental styles. Currently, the game looks like a prototype using placeholder assets from three entirely different asset packs (8-bit actors, vector UI backgrounds, and HD RPG props). To reach shippable status, you must establish *one* consistent environmental art style (either make the Prologue look like an abstract diagram, or make Array Plains a physical pixel-art space) and standardize the asset resolutions.

**4. Next single highest-leverage fix to push overworld past 3.5**
**[ASSET FIX]: Purge and replace every remaining HD/painted prop in the overworld.** 
Specifically, redraw the mountain portal (`27b-twin-rivers-closure.png`) and the sealed stone doors/glowing chains (`13b-prologue-return.png`) as flat, 8-bit pixel art assets. They must strictly adhere to the player sprite's low resolution, limited color palette, and flat shading complexity. Removing the HD clash is the fastest way to stabilize the visuals.
