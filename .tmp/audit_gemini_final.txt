### SPECIFIC ASKS

**1. Did Wave 4 fix A2+A3? If yes, by how much? If no, what specifically still fails?**
No, it failed spectacularly. You replaced an overly-detailed AI hallucination with an overly-simplistic one without adjusting the world to match. 
- **A2 Fails:** You cannot mix "chunky" 16x16 upscaled sprites with fine-detail 32x32/64x64 backgrounds and props. 
- **A3 Fails:** The scale is entirely arbitrary. The Village Elder (`13`) is huge. The player is tiny. The portals are mid-sized. Nothing feels anchored to a master grid.

**2. CRITICAL: Does the player sprite (v8) read as 16x16-effective?**
Yes, the player sprite *itself* reads as 16x16. **But it is scaled up massively and dropped into a world that is NOT 16x16 effective.** In `03-prologue-settled.png`, the player's 16x16 grid is stretched so large that one player "pixel" covers roughly sixteen background pixels. It looks incredibly cheap. 

**3. CRITICAL: Which screenshot is now the WORST?**
**`13-array-plains-marker-panel.png`**. It is a masterclass in visual incoherence. In one single frame, you have:
1. The Village Elder (HD, soft shading, realistic proportions, high pixel density).
2. The Player (ultra-low-fi, flat color, super-deformed proportions, massive chunky pixels).
3. The Portal (mid-fi, metallic hard shading, medium pixel density).
4. The Background (vector-style gradient lines with zero pixel texture).
It looks like four different games dragged-and-dropped onto a canvas. 

**4. CRITICAL: Which screenshot is the new BEST?**
**`04-p0-1-layout.png`**. Because it strips away all of your mismatched, uncurated AI assets. The vector UI, the simple geometric shapes, and the clean text on a starfield actually look intentional and designed. When you stop trying to force incompatible pixel art together, the game looks okay.

**5. REGEN LIST (Priority Order)**

1. **`player_walk_sheet_v8.png` (and all variants)**
   - **Failure:** "Pokemon Red" flat 5-color sprites DO NOT WORK on shaded, detailed backgrounds. The chunky pixels clash with the fine floor textures (`03`).
   - **Guidance:** If you are keeping the detailed floors, the player MUST be rendered with matching pixel density and shading. Look at the shading on the stone pillars in the prologue. The player needs that level of detail, lighting, and pixel size.

2. **All NPC Sprites (Village Elder, Sorting Farmer, Professor Node, Rune Keeper, Console Keeper)**
   - **Failure:** Wildly inconsistent scale and style. Village Elder (`13`) and Sorting Farmer (`08`) are massive HD sprites. Professor Node (`14`) and Rune Keeper (`15`) are flat, unshaded blobs with chunky pixels.
   - **Guidance:** Establish a strict, global bounding box (e.g., 32x32 native pixels) and a strict shading rule for ALL characters. They must look like they belong to the same species and hardware generation.

3. **`assets/twin_rivers/mirror_serpent_portal.png` (visible in 27b)**
   - **Failure:** A brown rocky archway with pine trees sitting on a flat, abstract mint-green UI grid. It looks completely hallucinated and imported from an RPG Maker fantasy tile set. 
   - **Guidance:** If the Array Plains/Twin Rivers overworld is an abstract grid, the portals must be abstract/geometric constructs, not literal rocks and trees.

4. **`assets/twin_rivers/mirror_walk_tower.png` (visible in 22)**
   - **Failure:** A highly detailed, shaded pixel-art tower with splashing water, resting on a flat, solid-color green box.
   - **Guidance:** Unify the environment style. Either the floor gets texture and detail to support the tower, or the tower is simplified to a flat geometric icon to match the floor.

**6. Ship verdict: At this score, is the game shippable? If not, what's the gap to shippable (3.5/5)?**
Absolutely not shippable. To reach 3.5/5, you have to stop patching individual assets and establish a **Global Art Bible**. You need a strict rule for:
1. **Screen Pixel Ratio:** 1 sprite pixel must equal exactly 1 background pixel. 
2. **Shading Rules:** If the world has cast shadows and gradients, the characters must have cast shadows and gradients.
Right now, you are throwing AI prompts at the wall and hoping they stitch together. They don't. Establish a baseline resolution and enforce it ruthlessly.
