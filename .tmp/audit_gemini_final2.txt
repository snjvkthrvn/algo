### Specific Asks

**1. Did Wave 4 fix A2+A3?**
No. It swapped one extreme for another. You replaced high-res HD models with extreme low-res 16x16 sprites, but you didn't scale the world to match. The result is "mixels" — the characters have massive, chunky pixels while the environment and props (like the altars) have small, detailed pixels.

**2. CRITICAL: Does the player sprite (v8) read as 16x16-effective?**
Yes, but the world reads as 64x64. Look at `03-prologue-settled.png`: the player's single black outline pixel is visibly 3-4 times thicker than the dark outline on the floor tiles. In `08-array-plains-continue.png`, the chunky 16x16 player is placed on top of a perfectly smooth vector gradient background. It looks like developer placeholder art.

**3. CRITICAL: Which screenshot is now the WORST?**
`27b-twin-rivers-closure.png`. It is a masterclass in art style collision. You have the giant Atari-pixel player, standing on a smooth vector gradient floor, next to an HD, AI-generated, softly shaded mountain arch portal with pine trees. It is three completely incompatible game eras mashed into one frame.

**4. CRITICAL: Which screenshot is the new BEST?**
`06-p0-1-player-turn.png`. Because it entirely drops the overworld and commits to the clean, vector UI aesthetic. There are no clashing pixel sizes here, just a consistent sci-fi interface.

1. **`portal_void_v2` / `portal_field_v2`** (seen in `27b-twin-rivers-closure.png`): The brown archway with pine trees.
